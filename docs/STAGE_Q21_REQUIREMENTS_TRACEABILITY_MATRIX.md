### Executive Traceability Summary

The Requirements Traceability Matrix maps every functional, physical, architectural, audiovisual, narrative, and platform requirement across all Seven Spheres (Lokas) of *Mythos* from initial launch to universal Moksha.

**Evaluation Taxonomy:**
- **VERIFIED:** Requirement fully validated through automated in-engine test execution, physics assertions, and rendered visual audit.
- **PARTIAL:** Requirement implemented and functional; advanced edge cases under continuous observation.
- **FAILED:** Regression or defect detected in active code path.
- **BLOCKED:** Dependency on unreleased hardware or external service prevents verification.
- **UNVERIFIED:** Untested or lacking runtime empirical evidence.
